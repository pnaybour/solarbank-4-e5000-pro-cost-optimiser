Automation

Automatic: the planner chooses the charge level.

Pause: the optimiser asks for no charging, so the Anker runs as normal; the advice is still worked out and shown. Override: the planner may use only your level for every cheap window until the end time (never below the minimum battery level). The controller acts on both; Neither sends commands while the controller is in shadow mode; the 45 A and car-charging protections are never paused.

Control and Anker app

Observe mode. No equipment writes.

Observe and Shadow both leave the optimiser in charge; whether it sends commands (live) is set on the Pi, not here. A handover request makes the controller cancel its commands, release the battery and leave it to the Anker app until you choose Observe or Shadow again.A handover request cannot confirm that Anker has regained control until release and readback have been tested. Automatic control is unavailable. The optimiser's current limiting and EV protection are not active.
