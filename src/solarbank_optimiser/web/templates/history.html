Daily savings and lost solar

Each finished day is scored from the one-minute record and your confirmed tariff. Estimates, not a bill. "Saving" compares what the household load would have cost with no solar or battery against what was actually imported. A day with missing minutes is shown but never added up. Lost solar is a candidate figure: it is only worked out when the previous evening's solar forecast was saved, and a forecast miss alone does not prove curtailment.

Download the half-hour record (CSV) for the dates in the form above: kept long term, aligned to Octopus half-hours; the minute-level detail is purged after seven days.

Agreement with the Anker (shadow weeks)

Each minute, what the Anker actually did (from the battery, grid and house readings) is compared with what the controller would have asked for. The controller sends nothing in shadow mode, so every difference here is information, not a fault. A minute agrees when: a requested grid charge was happening (or the requested level had been reached); no charge was requested and the Anker was not charging from the grid; and discharge was not being inhibited while the Anker discharged. Minutes with no controller record or no readings are counted separately and never as agreement.
