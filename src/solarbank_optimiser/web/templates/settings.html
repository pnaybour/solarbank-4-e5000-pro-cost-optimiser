Minimum battery level

The planner never plans below this level. The battery itself stops discharging at its own limit, which is set in the Anker app; the controller does not stop it. 10% is kinder to the battery than 5%.

How fresh a reading must be

The controller treats a reading older than this as lost. While a charge is running the garage current sets the charge power, so the limit for the garage current reading is tight. Every other reading, and the garage current when idle, uses the longer limit from the specification.

Battery calibration

The battery needs a full charge now and then so its charge meter stays accurate. Solar filling it to 100% counts. When it has not been full for nearly this long, the planner charges it to 100% in the next cheap-rate window whatever the cost-optimal level would be.

Device addresses

Where the Solarbank, the meter and the Shelly are on your network. If one moves, the controller finds it again by itself and tells you. To change an address yourself, type it here: it is used only if the device there is the one that was commissioned.

Household demand learning

Not available yet.

The planner can learn what the house really uses while you are away, and a multi-day average for normal days, but only after the learned figure has been better than the typed one over several days, and the Away figure never goes above what you typed. Switch it off to return to the typed figures and the last good day at once. It cannot operate equipment.
